## Deliverables

- A 5 to 7 minute video in English. Every team member appears and speaks.
- A deck of at most 12 pages in English. The deck is the only scoring artifact.

## Rubric (equal weight)

1. **Specific problem.** Name a failure in a named process. No general claims.
2. **Technical feasibility.** Show that the system can work.
3. **Business feasibility.** Include the regulatory reality in both countries.
4. **Mutual value.** State the value to Saudi Arabia and the value to China.
5. **Cross-cultural consumer.** Explain how behaviour, trust, and payment habits differ.

## What the rubric means for this repo

- The demo is the technical feasibility evidence. It must run, not just be described.
- The explanation layer is the cross-cultural argument. It must be readable by a non-specialist.
- The Sharia screen is the local-value argument. It must be explicit, not decorative.
- A number without a source is a scoring risk. Every number in the deck must trace to the engine or a cited source.
