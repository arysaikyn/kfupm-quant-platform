# Competitive landscape

## What exists

| Product | What it does | Overlap with us | What it lacks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WenCai** (Tonghuashun) | Chinese natural-language stock screen; rebuilt as LLM chat plus AI search this year | One sentence -> a list | It screens; it does not verify. China only. |
| **Composer** (SoFi) | AI builds trading algorithms -> backtest -> execute, with no code; USD 37B cumulative volume | Idea -> strategy -> backtest, the full loop | US only; an account is required; no teaching layer |
| **Sahm** (Saudi) | Saudi and US markets in one app; Sahm AI chat; Smart Selection and Halal Screening | Local market, Sharia screen, AI chat | No idea -> strategy -> backtest layer |
| **Magnifi** | Conversational AI for portfolio analysis and investment search | Conversational entry | Built for people who already hold assets; not a screener |
| **Danelfin** | Computes 900 indicators daily, scores each stock, explains the score | Complex analysis -> one readable conclusion | Gives a score, not a strategy |
| **Simply Wall St** | Fundamental analysis as a visual "snowflake"; 7M users | Professional analysis made legible | Company view only; no screen and no backtest |
| **Robinhood Agents** (September 2026) | Users build AI agents to manage investments; the platform also accepts external agents | A large player confirms that agents manage investments | US; an account is required; no Sharia rules |

## What we take from each

- **Entry:** a sentence, not a form full of terms.
- **Closure:** not a list, but a full test of the idea on history.
- **Local rules:** the Sharia screen runs before the ranking.
- **Explanation:** a conclusion a non-specialist can read, not a wall of indicators.

## The empty cell

Each part exists alone. The combination does not:

1. **Saudi Arabia has no "strategy plus backtest" product for retail.** Local products stop at "here is what this stock looks like". They do not answer "would this idea have made money".
2. **China has no retail product built around verification.** The natural-language screen stops at a list.
3. **No one runs one engine with two rule sets.** Products serve one market. Sharia screening is either absent or a static label.

Our position: put the verification layer in retail hands, and run it for two markets on one engine.
